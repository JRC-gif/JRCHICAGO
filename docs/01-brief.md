# Junior Chicago Website Redesign: Project Brief

Oct 19, 2026 · @Christian

## Overview

jrchicago.com becomes the home of Junior Chicago, a Chicago creative studio with Christian as its face, launching by **Nov 19, 2026**. The site does two jobs at once: it wins freelance clients and builds rapport with hiring managers. Both audiences want the same proof, so the site leads with work and credibility and sends everyone to one inquiry form.

**Goals**

- Turn the current two-page site (Home, Contact) into a portfolio with case studies, services, and an About page.
- Make submitting an inquiry the single primary action on every page.
- Launch a bold, creative brand identity built from scratch.
- Cut site costs from about $296 a year (Framer at $23/month plus a $20 GoDaddy domain).

## Audiences

Lead with manufacturers and CPG/retail brands, where retail merchandising know-how is rare. Hiring managers are served by the same proof. Local businesses and startups are secondary and will still see themselves in the work.

## Creative direction

All three references treat the website as an object you play with, not a brochure. The homepage should have one signature interactive moment, wrapped in bold, glossy visuals and very little copy.

| Reference | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| papertoilet.com (Rafaël Rozendaal, 2006) | One absurd, tactile interaction that is the whole idea |
| Cookie Clicker | Game-like interaction that rewards staying; dwell time by design |
| Jeff Koons | Art-world confidence, oversized glossy imagery, minimal text |

This direction also proves the work: Christian's strongest projects are interactive tools, so the site itself becomes a case study.

**Guardrails**

- The interaction never hides the work or the inquiry form.
- Fast to load, works on phones, readable by search engines.
- Headshot uses a placeholder until final photos are supplied.

## Platform decision

Undecided; decide by **Nov 1, 2026**. Brand, sitemap, and copy work are the same on either platform. The creative direction leans toward a coded site, because the signature interaction is easier to build in code.

| Option | Approx. yearly cost | Best for | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current setup | ~$296 | Nothing new | Overpaying for current needs |
| Framer Basic, billed yearly | ~$120 (a .com may be included) | Visual editing, quick copy changes | Custom interaction needs React code components |
| Coded site on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify (free) | ~$20 (domain only) | Full control, interactive features, true vibe coding | Edits go through code or Claude; form service needed |

Prices are approximate as of October 2026; verify on each vendor's pricing page before switching.
